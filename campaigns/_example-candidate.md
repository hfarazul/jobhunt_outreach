# About me

I'm a senior backend engineer with ~7 years building and operating high-throughput services. I do my best work on the parts of a system that have to stay correct under load — data pipelines, APIs, the boring infrastructure that everything else depends on. I'm looking for a senior or staff role on a small, high-ownership team where I can go deep on one hard problem rather than spread thin across ten.

# What I'm looking for

- Senior / Staff backend or platform engineering
- Product companies, ~20-300 people, past product-market fit but still small enough that one engineer's work is visible
- US-based or remote-first; comfortable with async teams
- Stack-flexible (strongest in Go / Python / Postgres) — care more about the problem than the language

# Proof points (use whichever fits the recipient)

- Cut p99 latency on a core API by 60% by redesigning the read path and caching layer — held through a 4x traffic increase
- Owned the migration of a monolith's billing system to a service without a single missed invoice
- On-call lead for a tier-1 service; drove incident rate down by half over two quarters
- Mentored three engineers from mid to senior; two now lead their own areas

# Anti-claims — DO NOT say

- Never lead with "I'm looking for a job" or sound like a cold applicant
- Never copy text from the job posting — referencing that a role exists is fine
- Never invent employers, metrics, or experience not listed above
- Never claim a specific tech stack the candidate hasn't actually used

# Tone

Peer-to-peer and direct. I'm a strong, selective engineer reaching out because the team's work is genuinely interesting — not someone hoping to be noticed. Concrete over effusive; engineer-to-engineer, not applicant-to-gatekeeper.
